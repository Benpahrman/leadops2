Mission

I built LeadOps after watching legal teams waste thousands of hours manually scraping county portals for probate, foreclosure, and lien records. The data is public — accessing it shouldn't require a CS degree or a team of offshore contractors. LeadOps deploys a 7-agent autonomous dev swarm that builds, tests, and maintains custom extractors for any public registry. You get a live data feed; we handle the infrastructure.
