Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know the data is real? +
Every sandbox provides 25 live, unmasked records extracted directly from the official public county registry. You can inspect case numbers, filing dates, and parties, and export the CSV to cross-verify against the official portal.
What happens if a county portal updates or changes? +
Our automated Retainer Monitor worker tests selector health before each morning delivery. Minor changes are auto-repaired in under 30 minutes; major structural redesigns are covered by our 4-Hour Selector Repair SLA at zero extra cost to active subscribers.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time? +
Yes. There are no annual lock-in contracts. You can cancel your monthly subscription at any time with 1 click directly in your customer dashboard or via PayPal.
Can we own the extractor code outright? +
Yes! We offer a Perpetual Code Buyout for a flat $1,500. You receive the complete self-contained Python / Playwright code repository (.zip), Dockerfile, and cron runners to host independently on your own servers.
